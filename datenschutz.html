3. Visitor count without cookies

To see how many people visit the site, I use the service GoatCounter (goatcounter.com). On each page view, your browser sends GoatCounter details such as the page you opened, the page you came from (referrer), your screen size, and your browser and operating system type. This only produces anonymous counts, e.g. page views per day and page. No cookies are set, nothing is stored in your browser, and no profile of you is built. According to the provider, your IP address is not stored.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in measuring the site's reach anonymously. Apart from this count, the site uses no analytics or advertising services.

4. TikTok names in the world

Ourtopia is a community story on the TikTok channel @ourtopia_world: everyone who comments under a video of the channel becomes a worker in an animated city. In the world, the search and the leaderboard, you see the TikTok usernames of these commenters. Each name is shown with a job in the story and the number of videos the person commented on.

The data comes from the public comments on TikTok. Only usernames are used: no comment texts, real names, profile pictures or contact details.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to make the community story real for its members: everyone who takes part under the videos should be able to find their worker, as the videos promise. Only public usernames of people who have taken part publicly under the videos themselves are shown.

5. Remove your name (objection)

You can object at any time, without giving a reason. Your name is then removed completely from the site with the next update, including the search. Use the "Remove my name" button at the bottom of the world, email tofelni@gmail.com or send a direct message to @ourtopia_world on TikTok. Requests are handled promptly, within one month at the latest.
